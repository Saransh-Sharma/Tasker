# Running Your Day

> Classification: Canonical user guide
> Audience: All LifeBoard users
> Capability status: Current workspace
> Source authority: [Home](../product/HOME.md), [Plan and Focus](../product/PLAN_AND_FOCUS.md), and Daily Loop runtime
> Last verified: 2026-08-11

The Daily Loop has five stages: Commit, Act, Repair, Close, and Rest. Moving backward to Repair is normal; it is not a broken streak.

## Morning: Commit

1. Open Home and read the date, daypart, Focus Now, schedule capacity, fixed calendar commitments, and care signals.
2. Open Plan → Day. Use Timeline when placement matters or Agenda for a compact reading order.
3. Navigate to the correct day and check working hours. Calendar events are fixed; internal time blocks and task placement are editable.
4. Choose a small set of outcomes. Use duration, energy, context, readiness, and project filters to find work that fits.
5. Leave capacity for transitions and care. A full bar is a warning, not a target.
6. If the plan is already unrealistic, use Replan or Minimum Viable Day before starting.

## During the day: Act

- Use Focus Now or open a task and start a scoped Focus session. Use an unscoped session for open-ended work.
- Choose duration, then pause/resume when interrupted. Record interruptions rather than abandoning the session.
- Finish with an outcome and optional reflection. Session history and rewards should record once even after retry/startup repair.
- Complete habits, routine steps, care events, and tracker values from the nearest safe surface. Widgets and Home rows call the same canonical mutation as the app.
- Capture new work immediately, but do not automatically place every capture today. Inbox protects the current plan.

Use search and filters to locate work; use the daily summary to see completion, remaining load, and signals. Navigate dates without changing the identity of the tasks being viewed.

## When reality changes: Repair

Open Repair from Home or Day when a meeting expands, energy falls, or overdue work crowds out the plan.

- **Replan:** move or unschedule work while preserving a planning receipt.
- **Minimum Viable Day:** protect essential care and one or two meaningful outcomes; defer the rest deliberately.
- **Overdue Rescue:** process overdue items one at a time or in a reviewed batch—complete, reschedule, defer, clarify, or delete.
- **Low Energy Home mode:** reduce visual and action pressure without deleting commitments.

Read the receipt before leaving a large batch change. If only part applied, LifeBoard should identify applied and unapplied items. Use Undo when the result is wrong; a failed change must preserve the prior plan.

## Evening: Close

1. Open Day Close and review completed, unfinished, deferred, habit/routine, Focus, and reflection evidence.
2. Correct inaccurate completions or tracker values instead of compensating with new records.
3. Carry forward only work you still intend to do; do not mass-roll everything to tomorrow.
4. Add a short Journal entry if context will help the weekly review. Mood and energy are optional evidence, not a grade.
5. Choose tomorrow’s first useful action only if that reduces morning friction.

## Rest

After Close, let the system become quieter. Respect notification quiet hours, avoid filling newly visible capacity, and use unstructured time intentionally. Missing a Close flow does not corrupt the next day; reopen the plan and repair from current reality.

## Common failures

- Calendar denied: plan with internal tasks/time blocks; reconnect later in Settings.
- Notifications denied: Focus/routines still work in-app.
- Offline: local actions continue; remote barcode/model/cloud work is labeled for retry.
- Stale Home card: open its source feature or refresh; do not assume the projection is current.
- Wrong completion: open history/detail and correct or Undo rather than creating an offsetting value.
