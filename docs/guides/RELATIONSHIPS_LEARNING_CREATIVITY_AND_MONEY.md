# Relationships, Learning, Creativity, and Money

> Classification: Canonical user guide
> Audience: Users balancing life beyond tasks and work
> Capability status: Current general-purpose tools; dedicated modules noted as future
> Source authority: [Feature Catalog](../product/FEATURE_CATALOG.md)
> Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Relationships

Use the Relationships life area for commitments you own: prepare for a visit, remember a date with a Life Moment, plan a shared experience as a project, or create a gentle check-in habit. Keep private observations in Journal or a secure note. Do not encode another person as a score, infer their consent, or expose their context on Home/widgets.

Dedicated people records, gift/care coordination, shared plans, and consent-aware collaboration are future. Current records remain personal and should not be presented as shared.

## Learning and growth

Create a goal for the direction, a project for a course or reading outcome, habits/routines for practice, and Notes/Knowledge for source material and learning notes. Link tasks and progress samples to the goal. Use Focus for practice sessions and weekly review to adjust difficulty or cadence. Search/indexed links help reconnect notes; an insight should reference evidence rather than claim mastery.

Courses, skill models, spaced-review queues, and competency evidence are future structured capabilities. Today, represent only what helps you learn without building a burdensome taxonomy.

## Creativity and fun

Capture an idea as a note when it is reference/draft material or as a task when the next action is clear. Use a project/board for a creative pipeline and protect time in Plan. Keep Home mode light and allow intentionally unstructured time; rest does not need completion evidence. Attach durable files and retain source/rights context in Knowledge.

Dedicated idea pipelines, events/leisure planning, collections, and protected-unstructured-time semantics are future. Existing projects, notes, moments, and time blocks are the current tools.

## Money

Use the Money area for responsibilities you already understand: pay a bill task, a monthly review routine, a savings goal with manual progress, or secure notes for non-secret reference. Do not store banking passwords or recovery secrets in ordinary notes. Keep financial context off public projections and attach it to EVA only intentionally.

LifeBoard currently does not provide account aggregation, transaction feeds, budgets, subscription management, or financial advice. The future blueprint requires explicit consent, provenance, export, and non-advisory language before those capabilities can ship.

## Review across these areas

Once a week, ask:

- Which commitment mattered, and was it represented at the right level?
- Did reminders support care or create pressure?
- What evidence is missing, and is it actually worth collecting?
- What should be paused, archived, or left unstructured?
- Is any sensitive information appearing on a projection or in EVA context unnecessarily?

Adapt the system to your life; do not adapt your life to maintain the system.
