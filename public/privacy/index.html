LifeBoard Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

What stays on your device

LifeBoard stores your tasks, plans, habits, journal, notes, and logs on your device. Health, mood, hydration, medication, fasting, and focus records are kept only on this device and are never uploaded to LifeBoard's servers.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, your tasks, plans, journal, and notes sync between your devices through your private iCloud account. Apple operates that storage; LifeBoard can't read it.

Apple Health

LifeBoard reads only the Health categories you allow, and writes only the water, nutrition, weight, and workouts you log, if you allow that. Health data is never used for advertising and never sold.

EVA, the assistant

EVA can run on your device, or in the cloud once you turn Cloud EVA on. Cloud EVA stays off until you agree to it. When it's on, your question and the context you allow pass through LifeBoard's cloud service (hosted on Cloudflare) to OpenAI to prepare an answer.

Before you turn it on, you choose which optional context EVA may use: journal entries, Health summaries, life moments, and what you've told EVA about you. You can change these choices, or turn Cloud EVA off, at any time in Settings › EVA. Health summaries are only sent if you allow them.

Product events

LifeBoard sends a small number of anonymous product events, such as whether a setup step finished, using an identifier that changes over time. They contain no task, journal, or health content. You can turn them off in Settings › Data & Help.

Permissions

LifeBoard asks for calendar, microphone, speech recognition, camera, photos, Face ID, and notification access only for the feature you're using, and works without any of them.

Your choices

You can delete your Cloud EVA account from Settings › EVA. Deleting LifeBoard removes the data stored on this device; iCloud data can be removed from your iCloud settings.
